Process

How I work now, and how I got here — from doodles in a school notebook to painting full pieces in Procreate.

The short version

Eleven years, one long sketchbook

I started drawing at twelve because I had a head full of imagination and wanted to put it into something real. It began as doodles in school. At sixteen I got my first iPad and started drawing in Autodesk Sketchbook, and around 2024 I moved over to Procreate, which is where I work now. Eleven years in, drawing characters — from moody portraits to my vampire-and-witchy side — is still the thing I most want to do.

Watch a piece happen

Two recordings, sketch to finish. No sound — just the drawing coming together.

Tools of the trade

What’s on the desk

An iPad and Procreate for the digital work, and pen and paper when I’m drawing live at the market. Autodesk Sketchbook is where I learned; Procreate is where I live now.
