Selected pieces

i. Illustration · Ink on toned paper

The Keeper of Small Hours

A familiar that only appears once the candles are low. Drawn entirely in dip pen, with the green wash added last so the ink would bleed the way old fear does.

ii. Painting · Oil

Fen-Light

Built from the bottom up in thin, cold glazes — the sort of green that hangs over marsh water at dusk and refuses to name itself.

iii. Digital · Painted

Vesper

A portrait made of screen-glow rather than daylight. Rendered to hold its atmosphere at any size, from phone to print.
